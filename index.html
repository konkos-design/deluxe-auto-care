What we do

Service details

Ceramic coating

Professional-grade ceramic coatings that lock in gloss and make every wash easier. Most coatings start with a polish, so the shine underneath is worth sealing in.

Paint correction

Multi-stage cut and polish that removes swirls, haze and years of automatic car-wash damage, and brings the clear coat back to a showroom finish.

Paint protection film

PPF for the areas that take the hits, or full-body coverage for a new car. Fitted carefully and finished cleanly.

Full detail, inside and out

Interior shampoo, seats, mats, vents and compartments done properly, plus a thorough exterior wash. Dog hair, salt and road tar included.

Maintenance washes and sealants

Regular maintenance details and winter sealants that keep salt and grime from building up on your paint or coating.

Headliners and starlights

Headliner work and starlight headliners for an interior that feels special every time you get in.
