Paint correction

Swirls, haze and wash marks dull even a new car. Multi-stage cut and polish takes the defects out of the clear coat instead of hiding them. One 2017 F-150 came in after seven years of automatic car washes and left with its showroom shine back.

  • Single or multi-stage cut and polish
  • Removes swirls, light scratches and oxidation
  • Brings faded colour back to life
  • Often followed by a sealant or ceramic coating
